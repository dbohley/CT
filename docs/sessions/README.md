| 009 | 2026-09-01 | [approach travel exhaustion](009-2026-09-01-approach-travel-exhaustion.md) | A run faulted with `no contact within 45s of approach`; the base had actually *arrived* at its 40 mm target at t=13 s and sat there for 32 s with no way to say it had run out of travel. Investigating found that **the rig had been operating on a 6% contact margin all along** — the one previously successful run contacted at exactly its 40.01 mm limit with a peak reading of 0.00941 against a 0.01 threshold. What tipped it over: **real subject profiles carry ~1.5 mm of slow baseline wander that a short looping profile does not** (emma is 618 s and completes zero loops in a 180 s run, so its played slice sat ~1 mm further out than breathing_profile_1's looped mean). Fixed by making `--travel-mm` an initial target that auto-extends to a new `--max-approach-mm` cap, with arrival judged from motor replies rather than dead reckoning, and failure modes that name distance instead of time. Threshold deliberately left at 0.1 mm: the pre-contact baseline maximum (0.0255 mm) already equalled the post-contact settled mean (0.0246 mm), so a lower threshold would have fired before contact. Also closes a question open since session 007 — **`measured_mm` is real at 51.1 Hz**, every tick, with the phantom tracking its own command to 99.1%. 313 tests. |
